Where it went

    Spending counts purchases and bills across all accounts, minus refunds. Card payments, loan payments and transfers between your own accounts are left out so nothing counts twice. Savings rate = (income − spending) ÷ income.

    Recurring money

    Found automatically: charges from the same merchant, about a month apart, at the same price (one price change allowed); paychecks and interest from regular deposits. Shared bills show your share. "✓ paid" bills this month are already in this month's spending. Loan payments and investing aren't spending, but they leave your accounts every month.
